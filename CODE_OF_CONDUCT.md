# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in CyberOS a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on technical issues, evidence, and reproducible behavior.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Help keep discussions welcoming and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method on the GitHub profile. Include links and relevant context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public spaces when someone is representing the project.
